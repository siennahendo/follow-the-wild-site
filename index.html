01 / PART OF EVERY DAYU.S. TEENS · 2025

This is where
they show up.

More than three quarters of U.S. teens use YouTube daily. TikTok and Instagram are daily destinations for a majority, too.

To invite young people into the natural world, our stories need to be part of the places they already spend time.

02 / BEYOND THE PHONEU.S. TV VIEWING · JULY SNAPSHOTS

The big screen
has changed
too.

YouTube accounted for 14.2% of all U.S. TV viewing time in July 2026. The worlds of television and creator video increasingly share the same screen.

There’s room for wildlife storytelling across short videos, longer films and live streams. The opportunity crosses formats.

01 / One wild idea

The wild has
incredible storytellers.
Let’s bring them together.

The next great wildlife story could be a YouTube collaboration, a TikTok series, an Instagram Reel or a live stream. We want to help creators make it together.

Follow the Wild exists to make the next generation care about wildlife and want to get outside. Our focus is the creator economy: supporting collaborative projects made for the platforms where young people watch.
