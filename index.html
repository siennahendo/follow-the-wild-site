Our purpose

More curiosity.
More connection.

Follow the Wild’s mission is to inspire the next generation of wildlife storytellers and foster a deeper connection between people and the natural world through education and storytelling.

A remarkable animal. A forest close to home. A changing ocean. We bring together the people who know these stories and the creators who can help others discover them.

What we’re building

Back a storyteller.
Open up a world.

01

Scholarships and mentorship.

Scholarships, mentors and creative resources for aspiring wildlife photographers, filmmakers and creators.

02

Storytelling for science.

Pairing emerging creators with scientists, researchers and wildlife professionals to document their work and share it widely.

03

Education and outreach.

Free wildlife and nature content, made with schools, educators and youth organizations.

Where stories meet people

In your feed.
In your world.

A short video can introduce an unfamiliar species. A live conversation can bring a field expert into someone’s home. A longer film can make room for a story that deserves time.

We’re interested in what each format can teach, and in the voices that help people understand wildlife, wild places and our changing climate.
