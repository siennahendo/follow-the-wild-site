Reading the evidence

Different measures.
One clearer picture.

Research reviewed 17 October 2026. Each chart names its geography, population, date and source. U.S. fans, U.S. teens and UK children are different groups. Discovery, monthly reach, daily use and time spent answer different questions.

The evidence shows where audiences and industry activity are moving. It does not establish that screen time causes interest in wildlife, or measure Follow the Wild’s impact. Our ambition is to turn those creative openings into curiosity about the natural world.

What this means for the wild

Attention is
an opening.
Connection is the goal.

These numbers show where audiences are. Our mission gives us a reason to meet them there.

By helping creators collaborate and funding the work they make together, we aim to put more wildlife, nature and climate stories into the videos and streams young people choose to watch.

The goal goes beyond a view: a young person who becomes curious, starts caring, and wants to step outside.
