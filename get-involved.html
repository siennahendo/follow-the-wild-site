A little more about us

Do I need a large audience?

We’re interested in ideas that connect younger audiences with wildlife. You can express interest at any stage. Eligibility for future funding opportunities will be shared when those opportunities are ready.

What kind of work is this for?

Our focus is content created for creator platforms: YouTube videos, TikToks, Reels, live streams and other social formats that reach younger audiences. Filmmakers and photographers are welcome to bring their skills into that work.

Am I joining a membership organization?

You’re registering your interest in our creator community. There’s no membership fee or funding application here. We’ll follow up about ways to connect and collaborate.

Can I apply for funding yet?

Funding applications are not open. We’re developing the first opportunities, including how projects will be selected and supported.

What’s the mission?

To make the next generation care about wildlife and want to get outside. Supporting collaborations on creator platforms is how we aim to get there.
