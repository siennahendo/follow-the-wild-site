01 / PART OF EVERY DAYU.S. TEENS · 2025

This is where
they show up.

More than three quarters of U.S. teens use YouTube daily. TikTok and Instagram are daily destinations for a majority, too.

To invite young people into the natural world, our stories need to be part of the places they already spend time.

02 / BEYOND THE PHONEU.S. TV VIEWING · JULY SNAPSHOTS

The big screen
has changed
too.

YouTube accounted for 14.2% of all U.S. TV viewing time in July 2026. The worlds of television and creator video increasingly share the same screen.

There’s room for wildlife storytelling across short videos, longer films and live streams. The opportunity crosses formats.

Why we exist

Wonder starts
with a connection.

A creature you never knew existed. A place you suddenly want to explore. A person whose curiosity becomes contagious. Stories can open the door to a lifelong relationship with nature.

We’re bringing storytellers together around content made for creator platforms, and working toward funding the projects they make together.

A shared purpose.
Many ways to tell it.

From wildlife and wild places to oceans, forests and our changing climate, there are countless stories worth caring about.

Our focus is content made for YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and live-streaming platforms. We want to support and recognize the creators using those spaces to connect younger audiences with nature.

We’re developing the first funding opportunities. The application process and timing are still taking shape; the purpose is clear: help creators make more compelling wildlife stories together.
