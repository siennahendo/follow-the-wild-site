First priority / The fellowship

A story to tell.
A little help to tell it.

A small grant can make a real difference to an emerging storyteller. So can time with someone who knows how to turn an idea into a compelling video, photo story or live conversation.

We’re designing a small first cohort, pairing project support with experienced mentors. We’re especially interested in storytellers with local knowledge, a distinctive perspective and a clear subject they want people to understand.

The fellowship is in development. Applications are not open. Funding amounts, eligibility, selection and timing will be announced when confirmed.

What we’re designing

More than
a grant.

01

Support for a specific story.

Help with the costs of making the work: fieldwork, production, editing or access to the right equipment, depending on the project.

02

Time with a mentor.

Practical guidance from an experienced storyteller on the idea, the craft and making work for the intended audience.

03

A plan to share it.

A clear format and publishing plan, with opportunities for collaboration and introductions to new audiences.

How we’ll report our progress

Show the work.
Share what we learn.

As projects launch, we plan to report the storytellers supported, mentoring delivered and work published. We’ll also share available audience measures such as views, watch time and engagement.

Those figures tell us how stories are being seen. Where a project includes a school session or another direct activity, we’ll report participation and feedback separately. Learning and changes in behavior need their own evidence.
