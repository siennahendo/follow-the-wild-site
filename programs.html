What we do

Four pillars.
One trail.

Fifteen programs that take people from caring about wildlife to protecting it. We launch a few first, then grow.

Inspire
Step 1 of 4

Spark curiosity

Wildlife films and stories that make people fall in love with the natural world, made free for classrooms and families.

  • Free Content LibraryFirst year

    Short, character-driven wildlife episodes and clips for teachers, families and anyone curious. No paywall, made for how people actually watch.

  • The Wild ClassroomFirst year

    Free, standards-aligned lesson plans and classroom-in-a-box kits built around our species films.

  • Explorers in ClassroomsComing next

    Live and virtual visits from filmmakers, photographers and scientists. Schools with the least access go first.

  • Ambassador NetworkComing next

    Creators, athletes, scientists and young people who share the work with their own audiences.

Explore
Step 2 of 4

Get outside

Turn that spark into time outdoors through challenges, kid-led projects, grants and expeditions.

  • Get-Outside ChallengesFirst year

    Family-friendly challenges that reward getting outdoors and spotting wildlife close to home.

  • Young ExplorersComing next

    Kids design and run their own local conservation or outdoor project, step by step.

  • Explorer & Expedition GrantsComing next

    Small grants that help young people and new talent get into the field.

  • ExpeditionsComing next

    Field trips that produce new stories and new science, with some trips open to supporters.

Discover
Step 3 of 4

Do real science

Help the public add to what we know about wildlife, and back the explorers and storytellers who push it further.

  • Citizen ScienceFirst year

    Log the wildlife you see. Every sighting goes into real datasets that scientists use.

  • Emerging Explorer SupportComing next

    Grants and mentoring for early-career field scientists, especially from under-represented regions.

  • Storyteller MentorshipComing next

    Training for the next generation of conservation photographers and filmmakers.

Protect
Step 4 of 4

Take action

Channel that connection into measurable conservation wins and support for the people working on the front lines.

  • Impact CampaignsFirst year

    One clear, measurable goal at a time, such as a newly protected area or a policy change.

  • Frontline GrantsComing next

    Funding passed straight to the conservationists doing the work on the ground.

  • Conservation PartnershipsComing next

    Brand partnerships where every purchase funds a set amount of conservation.
